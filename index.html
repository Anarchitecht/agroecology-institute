We do agroecological and permaculture design, creating sustainable human habitats with production systems modeled on natural ecosystems. We restore damaged soil and degraded land. We teach science-based methods for doing so, conduct applied research, and maintain a germplasm preservation facility. We develop new crop varieties by breeding plants for complex adaptive regenerative food production systems. We research the systemic limiting factors that block sustainable development, including the socioeconomic component, and develop solutions to overcome them for those wanting to participate in building an ecotechnic society.
